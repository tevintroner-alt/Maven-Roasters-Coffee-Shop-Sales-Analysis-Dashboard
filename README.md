# Maven Roasters Coffee Shop Sales Analysis & Dashboard

## Project Overview

Maven Roasters is a fictitious coffee shop chain operating across three locations in New York City. This project analyzes transactional sales data from the first half of 2023 to better understand purchasing behaviour, sales patterns, product performance, and operational opportunities.

The objective was to transform raw transaction data into an interactive Excel dashboard that franchise owners can use to identify patterns, compare locations, and make data-driven business decisions.

## Business Objectives

The project was structured around three core objectives:

1. **Profile and prepare the raw data** for analysis.
2. **Explore the data using Excel PivotTables and PivotCharts** to identify patterns and trends.
3. **Build an interactive dashboard** that communicates key findings and supports data-driven business decisions.

## Business Questions

The analysis focused on answering the following questions:

* How have sales trended over time?
* Which days of the week tend to be busiest?
* What times of day have the highest transaction activity?
* Do purchasing patterns differ across locations?
* Which products are sold most and least frequently?
* Which products and categories generate the most revenue?
* What operational opportunities can be identified from the data?

## Dataset

The dataset contains transaction-level records from Maven Roasters' three New York City locations covering the first half of 2023.

## Analysis

### Sales Trends

Sales were analyzed across the first half of 2023 to identify changes in revenue and transaction activity over time.

### Day-of-Week Analysis

Transaction activity was compared across different days of the week to identify the busiest periods and potential differences in customer behaviour between locations.

### Time-of-Day Analysis

Transactions were analyzed by hour to identify peak periods of customer activity and understand how purchasing patterns change throughout the day.

### Product Performance

Product-level analysis was used to identify frequently purchased products and understand which products and categories contribute most to sales.

### Location Comparison

The interactive dashboard allows users to filter the analysis by location, making it possible to compare purchasing behaviour and operational patterns across the three Maven Roasters stores.

## Key Insights

The dashboard revealed several important differences in purchasing behaviour between locations.

### Lower Manhattan

Lower Manhattan showed a particularly strong morning sales pattern, with a significant spike in transactions between approximately **7:00 AM and 10:00 AM**. This pattern is consistent with increased customer activity during the morning commute.

Monday also showed a particularly strong sales pattern at this location.

In contrast, transaction activity declined significantly during the evening, particularly around **7:00 PM to 8:00 PM**, suggesting that customer demand becomes relatively low later in the day.

At the product level, **espresso** was particularly popular, while **coffee and tea** represented some of the strongest product categories.

### Astoria

Astoria displayed a different purchasing pattern from Lower Manhattan. The Monday spike was less pronounced, and transaction activity remained relatively stable throughout the operating day.

The location also maintained more consistent transaction activity toward closing time.

At the product level, **chai tea** was one of the most important products, while coffee and tea remained among the strongest categories overall.

### Location Differences

The analysis demonstrates that customer behaviour is not uniform across the three locations. Differences in peak hours, day-of-week activity, and product preferences suggest that operational decisions should be adapted to individual store locations rather than applying the same strategy across the entire franchise.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Review Operating Hours by Location

The significant decline in evening transactions at Lower Manhattan suggests that management could review whether keeping the location open late is economically justified.

Rather than applying the same closing time across all stores, Maven Roasters could analyze sales and operating costs by hour and consider adjusting operating hours based on location-specific demand.

### 2. Align Staffing With Customer Demand

The strong morning demand in Lower Manhattan suggests that additional staffing and preparation may be required between approximately 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM.

Staffing levels could be adjusted according to expected demand throughout the day instead of maintaining uniform staffing levels.

### 3. Optimize Product Inventory

Differences in product preferences between locations provide an opportunity to optimize inventory.

For example, the strong performance of espresso in Lower Manhattan and chai tea in Astoria suggests that each store could prioritize inventory according to its specific customer demand.

### 4. Use Location-Specific Product Strategies

The analysis suggests that product mix should not necessarily be identical across all locations.

Maven Roasters could use location-level sales data to determine which products deserve greater visibility, promotional support, and inventory allocation at each store.

### 5. Review Low-Demand Periods

Periods with consistently low transaction activity could be used for activities such as:

* Restocking
* Cleaning
* Equipment maintenance
* Staff breaks
* Other operational tasks

This could help the business make better use of periods when customer demand is relatively low.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how transactional data can be transformed into actionable business insights using Microsoft Excel.

Rather than focusing solely on reporting sales figures, the analysis examines **when customers buy, what they buy, and how purchasing behaviour differs between locations**. These insights can support decisions around operating hours, staffing, inventory, product mix, and location-specific strategies.

The project also provides a foundation for progressing into more advanced data analytics techniques such as **Power Query and ETL, data modelling, Power Pivot, forecasting, automation, and Power BI**.
